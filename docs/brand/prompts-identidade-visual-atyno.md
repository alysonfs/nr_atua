# Prompts de identidade visual — hipotese ATYNO

## Status

Rascunho de apoio a [proposta-validacao-nome-atyno.md](./proposta-validacao-nome-atyno.md).
ATYNO e a hipotese de rename ativa (a proposta concorrente ATUVO foi
reprovada e o dominio `atyno.com.br` ja foi verificado e comprado pelo
responsavel do produto). Uma consulta ampliada ao INPI encontrou um pedido
de registro ativo para a palavra exata "ATYNO" (marca "ATYNO AGROSCIENCE",
classes NCL 01 e 35, pendente de exame) — ver detalhes e ressalvas na secao
"Consulta ampliada ao INPI" da proposta. Este documento nao representa
identidade aprovada: falta validacao de percepcao com usuarios e uma busca
de anterioridade completa com apoio juridico antes de qualquer decisao
final. So deve ser usado para gerar estudos exploratorios em Figma e Google
Stitch, nao para producao final.

## Contexto e restricoes

- ATYNO e uma hipotese de nome ainda nao validada; os prompts abaixo servem
  para visualizar a hipotese, nao para substituir a identidade vigente em
  [identidade.md](./identidade.md).
- A paleta de referencia (azul-marinho `#0F172A`/`#0E1A30`, azul tecnico
  `#3B82F6`/`#2563EB`, cinza ardosia `#64748B`, superficies `#F8FAFC`/`#FFFFFF`/
  `#E2E8F0`/`#F1F5F9`) foi reaproveitada como ponto de partida por continuidade
  visual com o estudo ja aprovado do ATUA, nao porque ja tenha sido validada
  para ATYNO.
- Principios visuais herdados de `identidade.md`: hierarquia clara, espacamento
  generoso, estetica corporativa moderna e minimalista, contraste profissional,
  sem decoracao que reduza legibilidade ou confianca.
- Estes prompts sao texto para colar em ferramentas de geracao (Figma AI /
  Google Stitch). Nenhuma imagem foi gerada por este time.

## Prompt 1 — Estudo de logotipo (fundo claro e escuro)

```text
Design a modern, minimalist wordmark logo for a B2B SaaS brand named "Atyno".
Style: corporate, technical, trustworthy, clean sans-serif typography with
confident letterforms. The name comes from the Portuguese word "atinar"
(to pinpoint, to figure out precisely) with a stylized "y", so the mark should
feel precise and sharp, not playful or decorative.
Color palette: deep navy #0F172A and #0E1A30 as primary dark tones, technical
blue #3B82F6 and #2563EB as accent/action color, slate gray #64748B for
secondary elements. Avoid gradients that reduce legibility.
Deliver two variants:
1. Light background version: wordmark in deep navy #0F172A on white #FFFFFF
   or light surface #F8FAFC, with an optional small technical-blue accent
   mark (e.g., a dot, connector node, or convergence-point motif) integrated
   subtly into a letter. Do not use crosshair, scope, target, bullseye or any
   weapon-related imagery.
2. Dark background version: wordmark in white or light surface #F8FAFC on
   deep navy #0F172A background, same accent treatment in technical blue
   #3B82F6.
Keep the mark simple enough to work at small sizes (favicon, app icon) and
large sizes (banners, signage). No literal industrial/HVAC icons, no gradients
that clash with a flat corporate palette, no cursive or handwritten styles.
```

## Prompt 2 — Aplicacao em papelaria (business card, letterhead, envelope)

```text
Create a corporate stationery set mockup (business card, letterhead, envelope)
for a B2B SaaS company named "Atyno", using the wordmark and accent style
described for the Atyno brand study (deep navy #0F172A / #0E1A30 primary,
technical blue #3B82F6 / #2563EB accent, slate gray #64748B secondary text,
light surfaces #F8FAFC / #FFFFFF / #E2E8F0 / #F1F5F9).
Business card: horizontal layout, logo top-left on a light surface background,
name and role in slate gray, a thin technical-blue rule or corner accent,
generous white space, no illustrations.
Letterhead: logo top-left, deep navy header band optional, clean body area for
text, footer with contact details in small slate-gray type.
Envelope: logo top-left small, minimal, no additional graphics.
Overall mood: precise, confident, technical, professional — consistent with a
platform that organizes technical service operations. Avoid playful icons,
avoid stock-photo textures, avoid HVAC/refrigeration clip-art.
```

## Prompt 3 — Banner para Facebook

```text
Design a Facebook cover banner (820x312px, keep central safe zone clear of
profile picture overlap) for a B2B SaaS brand named "Atyno" — a platform that
connects the operations of technical service companies (data, processes and
field work in one place).
Background: deep navy #0F172A or #0E1A30, optionally with a subtle abstract
pattern suggesting connected data points or converging flow lines (inspired by
rivers/waterways converging, used only as a subtle geometric texture, not a
literal illustration).
Typography: wordmark "Atyno" in white or light surface #F8FAFC, technical blue
#3B82F6 accent on a short tagline or connector element.
Keep it clean, corporate, minimal — no stock photography of people, no
HVAC/refrigeration equipment imagery, no clutter. Leave clear negative space
around where the profile picture will overlap on the left side.
```

## Prompt 4 — Banner para Instagram (destaque/capa de historia ou post de perfil)

```text
Design an Instagram profile highlight cover and a square post template
(1080x1080px) for a B2B SaaS brand named "Atyno", a platform that organizes
the operations of technical service companies.
Style: corporate minimalist, deep navy #0F172A background, technical blue
#3B82F6 / #2563EB accents, slate gray #64748B for secondary text, light
surface #F8FAFC for contrast elements.
Highlight cover: simple icon-only version of the Atyno mark (or an initial
"A" monogram with a connector-node accent), centered, no text, works at
small circular crop. Do not use crosshair, scope, target, bullseye or any
weapon-related imagery.
Square post template: wordmark centered or top-aligned, generous white space,
one short line of supporting text placeholder in slate gray, subtle geometric
accent (connector nodes or flow lines), no photography, no icons unrelated to
data/operations/technology.
```

## Prompt 5 — Banner para WhatsApp Business (foto de perfil e imagem de destaque)

```text
Design a WhatsApp Business profile picture (square, works well cropped to a
circle, 640x640px) and a WhatsApp channel/status banner for a B2B SaaS brand
named "Atyno".
Profile picture: icon-only mark or monogram "A" with a subtle connector-node
or convergence-point accent, deep navy #0F172A background or white #FFFFFF
background with deep navy mark, technical blue #3B82F6 as single accent color.
Do not use crosshair, scope, target, bullseye or any weapon-related imagery.
Must remain legible and recognizable at very small sizes (as small as 48px).
Channel/status banner (1500x500px or similar wide format): wordmark left or
centered, short tagline placeholder, same palette as other assets, minimal and
uncluttered so it reads clearly on a mobile screen.
Avoid decorative elements, avoid photography, keep contrast high for
accessibility.
```

## Observacoes finais para quem for colar os prompts

- Gere as duas versoes (fundo claro e fundo escuro) do Prompt 1 antes de
  aplicar os demais prompts, para manter consistencia entre as pecas.
- Se o resultado do Figma/Stitch usar uma paleta diferente da listada aqui,
  ajuste manualmente as cores no prompt antes de gerar novamente — as
  ferramentas de geracao de imagem nem sempre respeitam hex codes com
  precisao.
- Estes ativos sao apenas para avaliacao interna da hipotese ATYNO. Nenhuma
  pagina publica, papelaria real ou canal social deve ser atualizado com eles
  antes de uma decisao formal de rename.
